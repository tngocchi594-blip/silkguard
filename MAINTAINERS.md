The primary maintainer currently owns:

- issue triage and contributor support;
- pull-request review and release management;
- CI, dependency, and security-maintenance workflows;
- review of rule-pack provenance, effective dates, and regression tests;
- enforcement of the boundary between deterministic controls, AI explanations, and human decisions;
- removal of credentials, customer documents, and unlicensed data from public history.

## Becoming a maintainer

Regular contributors may be invited to maintain a defined area after demonstrating sustained, evidence-conscious contributions. Maintainers must follow the security policy, disclose relevant conflicts, and avoid presenting project output as legal advice or certification.

## Project status

SilkGuard is an early public preview. Maintenance activity is recorded through commits, issues, pull requests, Actions, security advisories, and tagged releases. Project adoption metrics will be reported only when they can be verified from public or consented usage data.
