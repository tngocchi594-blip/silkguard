# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, evidence, code, and outcomes.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's confidential information.
- Avoid stigmatizing claims about countries, cultures, communities, or users.
- Accept maintainer decisions about safety, evidence quality, and project scope.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions that violate these expectations. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub's available private contact mechanisms. Maintainers will handle reports confidentially and proportionately.

This code of conduct is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
